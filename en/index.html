A closer look at the rules and the philosophy behind the game.

Win condition

Four slots. One AGI.

To ignite the AGI your rig needs four healthy components across four distinct categories — Hardware, Dataset, Neural Net and Algorithm — with one healthy card per slot. The Quantum Core wildcard counts as any missing slot. The first player to assemble four healthy components wins immediately.

Turn cycle

Play or purge. Fifteen seconds.

Every turn gives you 15 seconds to play exactly 1 card onto any rig, or discard 1 to 3 cards to purge your grip. Your hand auto-refills to 3 cards from the shared deck. Fast turns keep the duel aggressive and responsive.

Interference

Threats, defenses, protocols.

Infect rival components with threats to block their win, shield your own slots with defenses to lock them permanently, or deploy game-changing protocols like Data Heist and System Override. Every move balances assembly against sabotage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI Sabotage.

What is AI Sabotage: Cyber Cards?

AI Sabotage is a fast-paced cyberpunk strategy card game for iOS and Android where up to 4 players duel to construct an AGI rig across four component categories: Hardware, Dataset, Neural Net, and Algorithm. Each match lasts three minutes, combining set collection with cyber sabotage mechanics.

How do you win a match?

You win immediately by assembling four different healthy components on your rig, or by using the Quantum Core wildcard to substitute for any missing slot. Healthy components have no active threats attached.

Can I play offline without internet?

Yes. AI Sabotage features a full single-player mode against CPU with an interactive tutorial, as well as a local hot-seat mode for 2–4 players on a single device without an internet connection.
