[ 02 // DECK ]

Four categories. Endless lines.

Draw from a shared deck of components, threats, defenses and protocols. Every draw reshapes the field and forces a new plan in seconds.

[ 02.1 // COMPONENTS ]

Components

The foundational components of your AGI: Hardware, Dataset, Neural Net, Algorithm, plus the Quantum Core wildcard. Assemble four healthy ones on your rig to win.

[ 02.2 // THREATS ]

Threats

Malware targeting rival components. One threat infects a component; a second threat of the same suit permanently destroys the entire stack.

[ 02.3 // DEFENSES ]

Defenses

Countermeasures and shields. One defense protects a component by absorbing the next threat; a second defense permanently locks the component.

[ 02.4 // SPECIAL PROTOCOLS ]

Special Protocols

Instant action programs that rewrite the board: Data Heist, EMP Purge, System Override, Protocol Swap, Malware Migration, and System Purge.

Tactical synergies

The shared 54-card deck contains components, threats, defenses, and protocols across four distinct suits, plus the Quantum Core wildcard. Every card plays a tactical role: components advance your win condition, threats disrupt rival builds, defenses shield your infrastructure, and protocols execute board-wide interference.

Locking a slot

Stacking two defenses onto a component locks it permanently. A locked component is completely immune to threats and can only be shifted by a full System Override.

Curing & neutralizing

Playing a defense on an infected component cures it, discarding both threat and defense. Playing a threat on a protected component destroys the shield, leaving the component vulnerable.

Protocol power plays

Protocols trigger instant board rewrites: deploy Data Heist to steal an un-locked component, EMP Purge to wipe all rival hands, or System Override to invert entire rigs when behind.
