Ranked competition takes place in online public matches. Every authenticated victory awards +10 PTS, while abandoning a match mid-game deducts 5 PTS (with a floor of 0). Offline games and private friend matches never award or penalize points. Compete against operators worldwide on the live ranking ladder.
