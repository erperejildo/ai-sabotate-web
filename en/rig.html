Infect, protect, fortify

Threats infect rival components; a second threat of the same color permanently destroys the stack. Defenses protect your slots from incoming malware, and a second defense locks the component permanently against threats. Quantum Core serves as a universal wildcard, making it the highest-priority target on the board.
